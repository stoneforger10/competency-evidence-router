> CompetencyEvidenceRouter replaces two parallel graph-ordering submissions with one learner-driven evidence-to-action primitive. PASS advances the learner; FAIL records RETRY without advancement; unavailable or mismatched evidence records INCONCLUSIVE. Attempts are immutable and bound to contract, program, learner and module.
